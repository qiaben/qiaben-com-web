### How to Customize OpenEMR (Step-by-Step)

#### Step 1: Add Specialty Modules

[**OpenEMR**](https://wiki.open-emr.org/) provides plugins and modules that you can activate:  
Dental charting tools  
Dermatology photo upload features  
Pediatric growth chart integration

#### Step 2: Create Custom Forms

Go to Administration > Forms > Layout and begin creating the forms you want. I mean, think intake forms, SOAP notes, or specific treatment records.

#### Step 3: Set Up Decision Rules

Leverage Clinical Decision Rules as prompts for routine rechecks or alerts, such as for skin mole checks in the field of dermatology.

#### Step 4: Customize Billing

Update your billing sheets and templates with the correct CPT and ICD-10 codes. That means your billing adds up, and you’re strip-free.
