#### The Drive Towards AI and Automation

Among the most exciting new technologies in the dental billing and insurance claims processing business is the marriage of business intelligence (BI) software and robotic process automation (RPA). These technologies are revolutionizing the process of submitting, tracking, and following up on claims. Providers who have AI systems can predict claim outcomes, alert potentially risky AR accounts, and reduce days in accounts receivable by up to 50%.
