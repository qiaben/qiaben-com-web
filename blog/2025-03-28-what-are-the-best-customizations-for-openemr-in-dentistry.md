## Introduction

In the fast-evolving digital revolution swallowing the dental practice, dentists in most dental clinics seek means of scaling their administrative and clinical procedures through the support of **[OpenEMR dental customization](https://qiaben.com/exploring-openemrs-core-features-what-you-need-to-know/).** An open source electronic medical record EMR software extremely adaptable to meet the specific needs of dental practitioners. ## Why Dental Offices Need OpenEMR Customization

Dentistry is the only medical practice field with such advanced treatment planning, imaging, billing, patient record keeping, and workflow requirements. Prepackaged EMR software does not have the most common feature of dentists to allow for the efficient operation of a practice. Customization dental-specific EMR software bridges this gap. With OpenEMR installed in a workflow focused manner for the dental practice, practices can benefit from improved documentation, images viewed in real time, improved billing, and improved patient experience.

### Most Common OpenEMR Dental Clinic Customizations

#### 1 Dental Charting Modules

A complete dental charting module is perhaps the most critical component of any dentist EMR bundle. Graphical tooth charts are not included with base distributions of OpenEMR dental practice management but are provided with customization.

- Graphical Tooth Charts Double click to chart opens a tooth or surface to add findings and procedures
- Treatment Planning Allows aids to create modify and forward treatment plans in the future with dynamic charting templates
- Conditions and Procedure Mapping Map onto existing conditions like decay or missing teeth and procedures like fillings or root canals

#### 2 Personalized Dental Templates and Forms

Pre standardized documentation will decrease administrative burdens. Personalized dental templates and forms can be incorporated into OpenEMR dental customization so providers can document using pre standardized documentation.

- SOAP Notes for Dental Procedures Templates to mimic dental workflows
- Periodontal Charting Charts utilized to note gum status and probing depths
- Patient Intake and Consent Forms Individual forms to fill out in the dentists office to take sufficient medical history consent and insurance

#### 3 Interoperability with Dental Imaging Equipment

Care staging and coordination dependent diagnostic imaging employed by dentists. OpenEMR for dental clinics can be configured to interoperate with digital imaging hardware and software.

- DICOM and Non DICOM Imaging Intraoral camera photos radiograph storage and retrieval function and CBCT imaging
- PACS Integration PACS integration as an optional module
- Clickable Image Mapping Photo icons of visual within patient record for future documentation

#### 4 Dental Billing Customizations

Dental billing is much different from medical billing in the majority of situations. OpenEMR dental billing customization can be configured to handle dental care specific codes and claims needs.

- CDT Codes CDT code support
- Dental Billing Templates Templates for routine procedures such as exams cleanings crowns and bridges
- Claim Management Manual and automated processing of dental payers insurance claims

#### 5 Scheduling Enhancement for Dental Practice Appointments

Provider schedule case complexity and room are common ways of scheduling appointments in a dental office. OpenEMR scheduling module can be enhanced for these.

- Color coded Scheduling Different appointment types or providers color coded individually
- Multi chair View Multiple providers and chair control of treatment
- Automated Reminders SMS and email recall hygiene and appointment reminders

#### 6 Role Based Access for Dental Staff

OpenEMR customization for dentists can be user role configured to limit access to confidential information.

- Custom User Roles Role specific for dentists hygienists assistants and front desk
- Audit Trails Audit changes to all patient data
- Data Privacy Compliance Meet HIPAA compliant dental EMR guidelines  using restriction by job function

#### 7 Enhanced Patient Portal for Dental Offices

Simple to use educational OpenEMR dental patient portal increases patient engagement and supports communication.

- Treatment Plan View Allow patients to view their planned and completed treatments
- Instructions after Surgery Simple view of instructions after surgery
- Secure Messaging Allow patients to attach follow up information or reminders

#### 8 Inventory Management and Equipment

A requirement to keep dental equipment and supplies in good condition as well as an effort to keep the clinic open at all times.

- Tracking Inventory Log usage expiration date and reorder level
- Supplier Integration Approve orders and monitor shipping
- Maintenance Logs Monitor and track equipment maintenance

#### 9 Reporting and Analytics for Dental Metrics

All reports are fully configurable with real time insight into practice performance and compliance.

- Production Reports Monitor provider productivity by procedure or date range
- Case Acceptance Rates Monitor patient acceptance of treatment plans
- Insurance Aging Reports Monitor outstanding claims and maximize cash flow

#### 10 Telehealth Integration of Dental Consults

With telecare online the latest buzzword dental offices can take advantage of telehealth capabilities.

- Virtual Consults Provide virtual consults for second opinions or follow up visits
- Secure Video Integration HIPAA compliant video solutions integrated into OpenEMR
- Documentation Tools Take virtual visit notes and view patient charts
