### UNDERSTANDING EHR AND EMR :

- Electronic Health Records (EHR) and Electronic Medical Records (EMR) are essential technologies that support a modern [Patient Record Management System](https://qiaben.com/ehr-emr/) in healthcare organizations.
- These systems help healthcare providers digitally store and manage patient information such as medical histories, diagnoses, prescriptions, lab results, and treatment plans in a centralized platform. EMR systems are mainly used within a single healthcare facility to maintain clinical records and track patient treatments over time.
- EHR systems, however, allow patient data to be securely shared across different healthcare providers, hospitals, laboratories, and pharmacies, ensuring better collaboration and continuity of care.
