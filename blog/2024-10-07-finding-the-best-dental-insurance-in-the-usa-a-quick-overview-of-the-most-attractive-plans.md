## What is dental insurance?

Dental insurance helps cover the cost of dental care, like cleanings, fillings, and sometimes more complex procedures like root canals or braces. You pay a monthly fee (premium) and in return, the insurance helps cover some or all of your dental care costs.

## What types of dental insurance plans are there?

• PPO (Preferred Provider Organization): You can see any dentist, but you save more if you use one within the plan’s network. • HMO (Health Maintenance Organization): You need to choose a dentist within the network, and the plan covers basic services at lower costs. • Indemnity Plans: These let you see any dentist, but you’ll pay upfront and get reimbursed later.

## What is covered by dental insurance?

• Preventive care: Like cleanings, exams, and X-rays (usually fully covered). • Basic services: Like fillings or extractions. • Major services: Like root canals, crowns, or bridges (often partially covered). • Orthodontics: Some plans cover braces, but not all.

## What are some of the best dental insurance providers in the USA?

## How much does dental insurance cost?

The cost varies depending on the plan and coverage. On average, you might pay: • $20 to $50 per month for individual plans. • $50 to $150 per month for family plans. More expensive plans may cover a higher percentage of major treatments or have no waiting periods.

## What is a waiting period in dental insurance?

A waiting period is the time you have to wait before certain services are covered. For example, a plan might require you to wait six months before covering major services like crowns or root canals.

## What if I don’t have dental insurance?

Without dental insurance, you’ll need to pay the full cost of any dental treatments out-of-pocket. Many dentists offer payment plans, or you can look for discount dental programs that offer lower fees.
