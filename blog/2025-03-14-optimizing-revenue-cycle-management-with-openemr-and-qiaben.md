### Comprehending the Healthcare Revenue Cycle

Revenue Cycle Management is the process a healthcare provider uses to manage the entire financial cycle, tracking patient care episodes from registration and appointment scheduling to final payment of a balance. The healthcare revenue cycle consists of many moving parts including insurance verification, charge capture, coding, claim submission, denial management, and patient collections. A tiny blunder in any of the stages could mean delays in payments or complete claim denial in the future. With a system already burdened by regulation and staffing challenges, the last thing a provider wants is to deal with disentangled platforms or manual billing systems. This is why the need for a solid and thorough integrated system is needed.

### OpenEMR: A Versatile Basis for Billing Success

[**OpenEMR**](https://www.open-emr.org/) is an structured software that offers patient scheduling, electronic health records, e-prescribing, and a full medical billing suite. Moreover, OpenEMR has built in eligibility checks and provides suggestions on coding as the clinics are completing documents. Mistakes are caught at the source before they are costly rejected claims. OpenEMR handles electronic submission of claim, electronic remittance advice, and real-time reporting. The beauty of OpenEMR is that clinics do not need to engage with various systems for scheduling appointments, documenting material, and accepting payment--it's all connected in one place!

### Addressing Denials Before They Occur

Claim denials can be one of the greatest sources of lost revenue for medical practices. Claims can be denied for countless reasons, which could be extremely basic reasons, such as incorrect patient demographics, or more involved ones, such as missing prior authorizations or missing proper coding. The worst part is, a very large percentage of denied claims are never reprocessed, resulting in permanent revenue loss. Using OpenEMR and Qiaben, it eliminates many of these risks. The eligibility verification can be automated at the time of scheduling, which allows to confirm that the patient’s insurance information is valid, and allows for up-to-date insurance. Coding tools also flags any missing links between diagnosis and procedure(s), and Qiaben’s billing team checks every claim prior to submission. This will lead to cleaner claims, and faster reimbursements.  
  

### Revenue Strategies Based on Data

There’s no room for guesswork in today’s healthcare billing processes. Every financial decision must be based on solid data. OpenEMR offers dashboards and reports that present the real-time, financial metrics like claim status, denials, and accounts receivable aging. These financial indicators are critical for identifying inefficiencies and recognizing the services or payers that are holding up revenue flow. Qiaben works with practices to help relate this data to action so that process change can occur. As an example, if the dashboard displays that a particular insurance provider is taking a long time to pay for telehealth services, Qiaben can help practices find ways to optimize documentation or code usage to make sure claims are accepted.

### Mid-Cycle Revenue Optimization: A Mistake

Practices are typically focused on either the front-end tasks (patient intake), or the back-end tasks (collections), while middle of the revenue cycle tasks are ignored altogether. Ignoring the middle of the revenue cycle is an expensive mistake. Mid-cycle tasks, charge capture, clinical documentation improvement (CDI) and medical coding, are often where revenue leakage occurs in the revenue cycle. Qiaben focuses on mid-cycle optimization. Their audit team will review medical records to confirm not only were services provided, documented, and coded for. This reduces compliance risk and ensures practices get full reimbursement for all care delivered.
