### Common Reasons for Claim Denials in Healthcare

- Incorrect or incomplete patient information
- Expired or inactive insurance coverage
- Lack of prior authorization
- Errors in medical coding and billing
- Missing or inaccurate documentation

👉 Electronic Health Records
