### VistA: A Veteran-Proven System

The Veteran Administration’s Computerized Patient Record System (VA CPRS), better known as **VistA**, is one of the most respected open-source [**electronic health records software**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VistA) platforms. Widely used across the VA healthcare system, VistA has built a reputation for stability and adaptability.

Its success demonstrates that open-source EHRs can operate at scale and meet high satisfaction standards. While VistA may be more complex to implement in small private practices, it’s proof that free solutions can deliver enterprise-level reliability.

### Practice Fusion: Market Share in Small Practices

Practice Fusion is another strong example of how free EHRs have carved out market space. According to surveys, it holds **15% of the solo ambulatory market** and **12% of small practices with one to three clinicians**.

Designed with smaller healthcare teams in mind, Practice Fusion has become a popular choice for providers seeking cost-effective EHR solutions. However, it operates under a freemium model, meaning some advanced features and support come with additional costs.

### OpenEMR: A Global Leader in Open Source

**OpenEMR** is one of the most widely adopted free **electronic health records software** platforms. Certified by the ONC, it offers:

- Complete EHR functionality
    
- Scheduling and practice management
    
- Electronic billing integration
    
- Cloud or on-premises deployment
    

OpenEMR is flexible enough to run on Windows, Linux, or Mac OS X, and providers can choose between hosting it on their own servers or using a cloud-based setup. With over **15,000 global installations** and **3,000 monthly downloads**, OpenEMR continues to be one of the most trusted open-source EHR options available today.

### OpenMRS: Customizable and Community-Driven

**OpenMRS** is another open-source platform designed to allow healthcare professionals to build a customized medical records system. Its **modular, concept-dictionary structure** makes it highly adaptable and capable of supporting diverse clinical workflows.

Key strengths of OpenMRS include:

- Open-source code under the Mozilla Public License
    
- Strong interoperability and scalability
    
- Deployment on laptops, local servers, or cloud environments
    

With support from a community spanning **80+ countries** and **8,000+ sites**, OpenMRS stands out as a collaborative solution backed by global expertise. For mental health providers looking for customization and international community support, it’s a powerful contender.

### Hippocrate: Free EHR with Big Data Features

Hippocrate, developed by a nonprofit organization, aims to democratize healthcare technology. This free cloud-based EHR is particularly attractive for solo providers, with a paid option available for larger practices.

Its features go beyond basic records management, offering:

- Telemedicine capabilities
    
- Patient engagement tools
    
- Productivity and revenue analytics
    
- A secure, encrypted Medical Social Network for referrals and collaboration
    

Hippocrate is compliant with ICD-10 and SNOMED standards, making it suitable for practices focused on secure data handling and analytics. For mental health professionals, its emphasis on collaboration and patient communication tools may be especially valuable.
