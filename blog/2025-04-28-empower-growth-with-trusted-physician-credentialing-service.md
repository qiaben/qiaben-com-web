### What Is Physician Credentialing?

![physician credentialing](/img/uploads/2025/04/medical-questionnaire-1024x683.jpg)

Physician credentialing involves the thorough verification of a healthcare provider’s background, including their education, professional training, licenses, and employment history. Insurance companies, hospitals, and healthcare facilities use this information to ensure that doctors are properly certified and eligible to deliver care.

Without proper credentialing, physicians cannot legally provide services to insured patients. In other words, they can’t get reimbursed by payers, which directly affects the financial health of the practice.

### How Credentialing Works: Step-by-Step

Understanding the steps involved helps you see the full picture. Here’s how **[physician credentialing](https://neolytix.com/medical-credentialing-services/)** typically unfolds:

#### 1\. Gather Provider Info

Education, licensure, certifications, DEA registration, and malpractice insurance.

#### 2\. Application Submission:

This includes applications to insurance companies, hospitals, and networks.

#### 3\. Primary Source Verification

All credentials are verified directly with the source (e.g., medical schools, licensing boards).

#### 4\. Follow-Up

The credentialing team follows up on pending documents and approvals.

#### 5\. Approval and Enrolment

 Once verified, the provider is enrolled in insurance panels and can begin billing.
