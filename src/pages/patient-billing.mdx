## What is patient billing?

Patient billing is the process of generating and sending bills to patients for healthcare services rendered. It includes verifying insurance coverage, processing claims, posting payments, and managing patient statements to ensure accurate and timely payment.

</Reveal>

<Reveal delay={240}>

## Why is accurate patient billing important?

Accurate patient billing ensures that healthcare providers are reimbursed correctly and promptly for their services. It also helps prevent billing errors, reduces claim denials, and enhances patient satisfaction by providing clear and accurate statements.

</Reveal>

<Reveal delay={240}>

## How does Qiaben Health ensure accurate billing?

Qiaben Health utilizes advanced billing software and rigorous verification processes to minimize errors. Our team of experts ensures that all insurance details are verified before services are rendered, and we follow up diligently on any denied claims to ensure they are resolved quickly.
