## What are SOAP notes?

SOAP notes are a systematized format for medical records that organize clinical documentation into four sections - Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan - to enhance clarity, consistency, and coordination of care across a healthcare team.

</Reveal>

<Reveal delay={80}>

## How does Qiaben ensure Subjective and Objective clarity?

Qiaben guarantees accurate capture of patient-reported symptoms (Subjective) and clinical observations (Objective) in SOAP Notes, which facilitates more accurate diagnosis and treatment.

</Reveal>

<Reveal delay={120}>

## What are Assessment-Driven Insights?

Using Qiaben's SOAP format, providers can explicitly document clinical impressions and differential diagnoses to inform the treatment plan, keeping the assessment reasoning clear and traceable.

</Reveal>

<Reveal delay={160}>

## How do SOAP Notes improve care coordination?

The Plan section of Qiaben's SOAP Notes maintains the structure of the treatment plan and follow-up actions, establishing an efficient communication process within care teams.

</Reveal>

## Who benefits from using Qiaben SOAP Notes?

Any healthcare team looking to standardize clinical documentation benefits - SOAP Notes improve communication, reduce ambiguity in patient records, and support more effective treatment decisions.
