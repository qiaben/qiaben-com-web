## What is OpenMRS?

OpenMR (Open Medical Record System) is an open-source software platform that aims to facilitate health care delivery in developing regions of the world. It helps the healthcare professionals to collect, organize, and analyse patient information in a timely and secure manner.

OpenMR is developed by a worldwide community of developers, health workers, and non-governmental organizations (NGOs). Some of its features include:

- Modular and extensible
- Web-based and with offline features
- Scalable from small clinics to nationwide systems.    

 

OpenMRS is used in over 40 countries such as Kenya, Rwanda, Haiti, India, and Mozambique; it enables both humanitarian and public health projects throughout the world.
