## What is an Electronic Health Record (EHR)?

An Electronic Health Record (EHR) is a digital version of a patient’s paper chart. It contains comprehensive patient information including medical history, diagnoses, medications, treatment plans, immunization dates, and test results. EHRs are designed to streamline and improve the management of patient information within healthcare systems.

</Reveal>

## Why is an EHR system important for healthcare providers?

An EHR system enhances the efficiency and accuracy of patient care by providing healthcare providers with quick access to comprehensive patient data. It improves communication between different healthcare providers, supports better coordination of care, reduces errors, and helps in complying with regulatory requirements. Additionally, EHRs streamline administrative tasks, such as scheduling and billing, allowing providers to focus more on patient care.
