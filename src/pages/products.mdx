## Qiaben’s Placental Allograft Product Portfolio

### Palisade™ Placental-Derived Allograft

A dual-membrane graft combining **amnion and chorion**, ideal for moderate-to-deep wound beds.

-  HCPCS Code: Q4350
    
-  Sizes: 2x2, 2x4, 4x4, 4x8 cm
    

 **Key Features:**

- Excellent barrier properties
    
- Collagen types I, III, IV, V + ECM proteins
    

### Membrane Wrap™

An **amnion-amnion** graft with non-side-specific orientation, offering easy, sterile application.

- HCPCS Code: Q4205
    
- Sizes: 1x1 to 10x10 cm
    

**Benefits:**

- 5-year shelf life
    
- Ready for immediate clinical use

### Emerge™ Allograft Matrix

Triple-layer (amnion, chorion, spongy layer) design offering **comprehensive biologic healing** support.

- HCPCS Code: Q4297
    
- Sizes: 2x2 to 4x8 cm
    

**Highlights:**

- Contains key growth factors and cytokines (TGF-β, IL-10, bFGF, etc.)
- Supports host cell attachment and regeneration.

### AmnioAMP-MP™

A **chorion-free, dual-layer** amniotic graft with high tensile strength and excellent durability.

- Product Code: CG1100–CG1106
    
- Sizes: 2x2 to 4x8 cm
    

**Special Features:**

- Preserves ECM proteins like Collagen I, III, IV, V, VII, Elastin, Fibronectin
- Fast hydration and strong adhesion in surgical wounds

### Emerge™ Allograft Matrix

Designed for a **broad range of wounds**, Activate™ includes all three placental layers for enhanced performance.

- HCPCS Code: Q4301
    

Sizes: 2x2, 2x4, 4x4, 4x8 cm

**Benefits:**

- Loaded with signaling proteins and cytokines
    
- Great for inpatient or outpatient care
    

### XCell Amnio Matrix

A **lyophilized (freeze-dried)** graft optimized for chronic and surgical wounds.

- Product ID: XAM-0202 to XAM-0407
    
- Sizes: 2x2 to 4x7 cm

**Use Cases:**

- Diabetic foot ulcers
- Pressure wounds
- Reconstructive surgical applications

### Sentry™ Amniotic Allograft

A **single-layer amnion** designed for shallow to moderate wounds, with excellent flexibility and biocompatibility.

- HCPCS Code: Q4348
    
- Sizes: 2x2, 2x4, 4x4, 4x8 cm
    

Key Benefits:

- Elastic and compact ECM layer
- Simple, flexible placement on wound beds
